Privacy Policy

Where's My Shrimp? · Last updated: October 24, 2026

The short version

Where's My Shrimp? does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your tank details, livestock records, water test results, water changes, feeding, chemical and maintenance logs, notes, and any photos you choose to attach to an observation are saved only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. The developer cannot see this data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No network requests: the app works fully offline and does not send your data anywhere.

Photos

If you attach a photo to an observation, you pick it through the iOS photo picker and the app keeps a copy in its own storage. It is never uploaded.

Notifications

Optional reminders (for example for water tests) are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved.

Export, backup and "Ask AI"

Exports and backups are files created only when you ask for them, and you decide where to save or share them using the standard iOS share sheet. The "Ask AI" feature only builds a block of text from your own records so you can copy it. The app does not send it to any AI service. If you paste it into a third-party service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Your data is not stored anywhere else unless you export or back it up yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

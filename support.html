Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your device. Use Settings → Export, backup & restore to keep a copy before changing phones.

An animal I haven't seen is not marked dead. That's intentional. Shrimp and snails hide often, so unseen animals are tracked as "potentially missing". You record a confirmed death yourself. A "Dying" report counts as dead after 7 days unless you delete it because the animal recovered.

The Ask AI prompt. It only builds text from your records for you to copy. Nothing is sent from the app.

Water ranges. Defaults are general freshwater guides. Adjust them in Settings → Reference ranges to suit your species. The app is a logbook and is not veterinary or professional advice.
